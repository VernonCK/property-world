The Property World approach

Why independence matters.

Most investment decisions in property come with a built-in bias: the developer wants the pro-forma to work, the bank wants the risk to look small, the agent wants the sale to close. Our value is that we're on none of those sides.

Every opinion we deliver starts with the numbers as we see them — not as we wish them to be. We apply the same methodology the REIB uses for valuation, we test assumptions against real comparable stock, and we give you a clear, defensible answer to the question you're actually asking.

Whether you're an investment committee deciding on a P200 million infrastructure play or a family office weighing a small multi-residential scheme, our job is the same: give you an honest professional view, backed by evidence you can interrogate.

Independent of every side

REIB-grounded methodology

Our analysis follows the Real Estate Institute of Botswana methodology — the same standard your bank will recognise.

Numbers you can challenge

Delivered in weeks, not months

Common questions

How developers, investors, and their advisors usually engage us.

What does an investment consultancy opinion include?

It depends on the question you're asking. For a development, we typically deliver: market context (comparable supply, absorption, pricing), financial analysis (residual land value, IRR, yield on cost, sensitivity), risk assessment (planning, construction, financing, market), and a clear opinion on whether the numbers work at the proposed pricing. For infrastructure, it's a valuation of the asset or the pipeline against replacement cost and economic benefit. Every deliverable is written, cited, and defensible.

Who usually commissions these opinions?

Investment committees, family offices, private developers, pension funds, banks (as a second opinion on their own credit files), and government or parastatal entities evaluating infrastructure proposals. We work equally well as the primary advisor on a deal or as an independent reviewer of someone else's analysis.

How is this different from a valuation?

A valuation answers "what is this asset worth today?" — a point-in-time number, bank-ready, following the REIB scale. An investment consultancy opinion is broader: it looks forward, tests a proposed development or acquisition against market realities, and delivers a view on whether the decision makes sense. Many of our clients commission both — a valuation for their lender, and an opinion for their own investment committee.

What's the typical turnaround?

A focused opinion on a single asset usually takes 2–4 weeks from instruction. Larger or more complex engagements — a multi-phase development, an infrastructure portfolio review, or an independent challenge to an existing study — typically run 4–8 weeks. We'll confirm the timeline in writing before we start.

Can you review an investment plan that's already been done?

Yes — this is one of the most common reasons clients come to us. We're regularly asked to act as an independent second opinion on a pro-forma, feasibility study, or development appraisal prepared elsewhere. We don't rewrite the original; we test it, poke at the assumptions, and give you a clear view on where the numbers are solid and where they aren't.

Is there a fee for the initial consultation?

No. The first discovery call is free and without obligation. If you'd like us to proceed with a written opinion, we'll send a scoped proposal with a fixed or capped fee — no surprises, no hourly creep unless we've agreed it upfront.

Do you work outside Botswana?

Our core methodology is calibrated to the Botswana market, and most of our engagement is domestic. That said, we've advised on cross-border assets in the SADC region and can bring in partners where a specific local opinion is required. If you're unsure whether we're the right fit for a particular jurisdiction, mention it in your enquiry and we'll be candid about whether we can help.
