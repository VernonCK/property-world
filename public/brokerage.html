Common questions

What sellers and landlords ask before they list.

Sole Mandate vs Multiple Agency — which should I pick?

Multiple Agency means we co-list alongside other agencies — your property stays on the open market. Sole Mandate gives us exclusive rights for a set period (typically 3 months), after which it automatically reverts to multiple agency. Sole is best when you want a single, focused agency driving the sale. Multiple is best when you want maximum exposure.

What's the commission?

For residential sales: 6% of the sale price, based on the REIB fee scale. For commercial sales: 5% of the sale price. For leases: 1 month's rental — for commercial properties, this is deducted from the first rental payment. No upfront fees, no marketing charges.

When do I pay the commission?

At the close of the deal. For sales, commission is settled from the sale proceeds. For leases, it's settled from the first month's rent received. Nothing is due before a buyer or tenant is secured and accepted by you.

Can I list multiple properties in one instruction?

Yes — but each property needs its own instruction so we can present it accurately and register the correct mandate. Submit this form once per property. If you have a portfolio, email us at brokerage@pw.co.bw and we'll prepare a consolidated pack.

Do I need a valuation before listing?

Not required, but strongly recommended — it gives you an objective market value and makes negotiating faster. You can request a valuation alongside your instruction. Ask us for a bundled rate.

What if I want to cancel the mandate?

For a sole mandate, you're exclusive with us for the agreed period. If you need to cancel before then, contact us in writing — we'll discuss options. After the period ends, the mandate auto-converts to multiple agency and you can cancel anytime.

Is my electronic signature legally binding?

Yes. Your typed name, combined with the acceptance of commission terms and the automated timestamp on the PDF, constitutes a valid electronic signature. The signed instruction PDF — with your signature, date, and the REIB commission reference — is the authoritative record.
