Common questions

What landlords and body corporates ask before they appoint us.

What's included in the management fee?

Everything that keeps the property operating: rent/levy collection and arrears management, tenant and occupant relations, planned maintenance and contractor coordination, monthly financial reporting, and annual budgeting. There's no extra charge for routine management — only pass-through costs (like contractor invoices for physical repairs, which go to the property, not to us).

How is the fee calculated?

Per the REIB scale: 10% of gross monthly rental for residential, 5% for commercial and industrial. Sectional title is quoted per scheme because the scope varies considerably — send us the details and we'll give you a specific number. There's no setup fee and no minimum contract length.

What's the difference between full management and sectional title management?

Full management is for individual properties where you, the owner, hold the lease and receive the rent. Sectional title management is for schemes where a body corporate of owners holds the common property — we handle levy collection, reserve fund planning, compliance, and body corporate administration. Many clients come to us for both, and the two can sit under one engagement.

Do you handle maintenance and contractors directly?

Yes — that's a large part of the value. We maintain a vetted contractor network across plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roofing, and general maintenance. For routine jobs under a threshold (typically P1,500), we approve and proceed. Above that, we get your sign-off first. Every invoice is documented and shown in your monthly statement.

How do you handle arrears?

Our arrears process runs to a documented calendar: reminder at day 1, formal letter at day 7, phone contact at day 14, and legal escalation at day 30. Our target is to keep arrears under 5% of the collectable book at any time. We'll share the current arrears position of your portfolio on request — that's the number that actually matters.

Can you help with tenant vetting and new lettings?

Yes. Tenant screening (credit, references, employment), lease drafting, and move-in coordination are all part of the service. If we let a property we already manage, the letting fee is charged at 3% of the standard letting fee — a loyalty benefit for landlords in our care. Lease renewals are 1/3 of the letting fee or 5% of monthly rent, whichever is less.

What if I want to leave?

There's no minimum contract and no exit penalty. Give us 30 days' written notice and we'll hand over cleanly: full tenant records, leases, deposit ledger, contractor history, maintenance plan, and any outstanding arrears actions. We'd rather you stay because we've earned it than because you're locked in.

Do you offer advice on improvements and future purchases?

Yes — it's one of the four pillars of what we do. Once we're managing a property, we can advise on which improvements will preserve or increase value (and which won't), and on future purchases in the same market to maximise portfolio return. This isn't a separate consulting engagement — it's part of being a good managing agent.
