Other provisions that may apply
  • 1.2.1
    Additional valuations on the same property — charged at 5% of the first valuation fee. A normal valuation including the forced sale value alongside the open market value does not count as more than one valuation.
  • 1.3
    Prescribed forms — if a specific form is required in addition to the valuation, the fee may increase by 25%, up to a maximum increase of P250 per property.
  • 1.5
    Revaluation within 12 months — where the property has not changed materially, the fee is reduced by 50% of the first valuation.
  • 1.7
    "As is" and "proposed" valuations — charged separately per the scale, unless the second valuation does not amount to a completely separate valuation, in which case a single fee on the higher value applies.
  • 1.8
    Partial property or undivided share — where it is necessary to value the whole property to determine the value of the part, the fee may be based on the value of the whole property.
  • 2.0
    Time charges — for market research, deeds registry searches, or other investigations: P750/hr for a Fellow (15+ years), P600/hr (Fellow, <15 years), P500/hr (Full, 10+ years), P350/hr (Full, <10 years), P200/hr (Probationer, 3+ years), P150/hr (Probationer, <3 years).
  • 10.1
    Travel — per Property World's published instruction, travel in our own vehicle is charged at P5.50 per kilometre. Public transport and hired conveyances are charged at actual cost.
  • 11.0
    Disbursements — purchase and preparation of plans and drawings, external expert advice, typing and secretarial fees (15% of the time charge), postage, phone calls, photocopies and other incidentals are charged at cost.
  • 15.0
    Deposits — a deposit of P1,500 (within Gaborone) or P2,000 (outside Gaborone) is payable per instruction before we commence work. This is a payment on account of our fees, not a separate charge.

Common questions

A few things clients ask before they instruct us.

How is my valuation fee calculated?

Fees follow the REIB scale — a basic tariff plus an additional amount per P1,000 over the previous bracket, applied to the Open Market Value determined by our valuer after inspection. Use the calculator above for a quick estimate. The full fee schedule is published further down.

Is travel included in the deposit?

No. The deposit (P1,500 within Gaborone, P2,000 outside) is a payment on account of the valuation fee itself. Travel is charged separately at P5.50 per kilometre round-trip beyond Gaborone. Enter your round-trip distance on the form and the sidebar updates with the travel estimate.

What's the difference between OMV, FSV and RCV?

OMV (Open Market Value) is the estimated price between a willing buyer and seller, both fully informed. FSV (Forced Sale Value) is what you'd realistically realise under a forced sale — typically 10–30% below OMV. RCV (Replacement Cost Value) is the cost to rebuild from scratch, used mainly for insurance. Per REIB 1.2.1, FSV alongside OMV doesn't count as a second valuation — one fee applies regardless of which bases you request.

How long does a valuation take?

Standard turnaround is 5–7 business days from the date we receive your signed instruction and proof of payment. Complex or rural properties may take longer — we'll confirm the timeline once we've reviewed the file.

What if I need more than one property valued?

Each property is valued and charged separately per the REIB scale. The deposit is charged once per instruction, not per property — so instructing us on three properties at the same time means one deposit plus three valuation fees. Let us know in the notes field or via email and we'll prepare a consolidated instruction.

Do you charge extra for a revaluation?

Per REIB 1.5, a revaluation of the same property by the same valuer within 12 months — where the property hasn't changed materially — is charged at 50% of the first valuation fee. Beyond 12 months, standard rates apply.

Is my electronic signature legally binding?

Yes. Your typed name, together with the acceptance of the instruction terms and the automated timestamp, constitutes a valid electronic signature for the purposes of the instruction. The signed PDF — with terms, signature, and date — is generated on submission and is the authoritative record.
